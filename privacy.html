TimeGuardian — Privacy Information

This section applies to TimeGuardian (時間守護者), published by ChiYik Game. It is the applicable privacy information for TimeGuardian. The general descriptions for other ChiYik Game products below do not apply to TimeGuardian.

Information and purposes

TimeGuardian integrates Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app use and performance, and Google AdMob to provide rewarded advertising. Depending on your choices, device settings and service configuration, these services may process app interactions, technical device and app information, IP addresses, advertising or app-instance identifiers, and advertising interactions. Some information may be used to infer an approximate location. These data are not necessarily anonymous. References below to services such as Crashlytics or AWS do not describe TimeGuardian integrations.

Advertising and analytics choices

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect and manage applicable consent choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Google consent form offers choices for advertising and analytics purposes. You may decline or manage the available purposes. Declining consent does not prevent you from playing the core game; ad availability can depend on your choices and Google's requirements. Choosing to watch a rewarded ad is separate from consenting to data use.

Defaults and limited measurement

TimeGuardian configures analytics storage, advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization as denied by default. Google's SDKs retain and apply previously recorded choices where applicable. If UMP does not update consent for your region, these defaults remain denied. We use advanced Consent Mode: a denied status does not mean that all network communication stops. Google may receive limited, cookieless measurement signals in accordance with its Consent Mode behavior.

Changing your choices

When Google requires a privacy options entry point, you can reopen its form from Settings → Ads privacy options (設定 → 廣告隱私選項) in the game. Updating a choice affects subsequent processing; it does not by itself erase data already processed.

Service providers and international transfers

Google processes data for analytics and advertising under its applicable policies. Data may be processed outside your country. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. The consent form provides the applicable advertising-partner disclosures.

Retention

Our TimeGuardian Google Analytics property is configured to retain event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. New user activity resets the user-level retention period, so continued activity can extend it. These settings do not mean that all information is deleted after these periods: standard aggregated reports are not generally affected, and Google's advertising and other service data are subject to the applicable service policies. See Google Analytics data retention.

Your requests and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your local supervisory authority. For TimeGuardian privacy questions or requests, contact r93549022@gmail.com. Describe your request and the app concerned; do not send passwords or payment credentials. We may need information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant data and verify a request. Data handled by Google may also be subject to Google's request procedures.

Children and intended audience

TimeGuardian is intended for people aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through TimeGuardian, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable. This intended audience does not replace any higher consent age or parental-consent requirements applicable in your location.

Policy updates

We may update this information as the app or its services change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest update.
